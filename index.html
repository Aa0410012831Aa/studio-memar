Saeid
Shahamat

Founder & Principal Architect — Studio Memar S.r.l.

Born on May 6, 1961 in Esfahan, Iran, Saeid Shahamat carried the imprint of Persian architectural tradition when he moved to Italy in 1984. At the Istituto Universitario di Architettura di Venezia, he encountered the Renaissance masters — Palladio and Sansovino — and recognised profound parallels with the heritage he had grown up admiring.

This synthesis of Eastern and Western architectural thinking became the cornerstone of his practice. His thesis, guided by Prof. Giorgio Lombardi, focused on urban requalification — a discipline that would define much of Studio Memar's most significant work.

The study of contemporaries such as Aldo Rossi and Mario Botta refined his understanding of materiality, while a deep interest in minimalism — traced through Mies van der Rohe — shaped his approach to modern construction. Today, Studio Memar operates across Italy and Iran, with an international portfolio spanning restoration, residential, commercial, urban planning, and sports infrastructure.

"In every ancient building lies a suggestion for the construction of a contemporary architecture — one need only know how to read it."

— Saeid Shahamat, on his design philosophy

Design Philosophy

Where Persian heritage meets Italian Renaissance — a dialogue across centuries.

Studio Memar's work is grounded in the belief that architecture must honour its context — historical, cultural, and natural. Each project begins with a rigorous reading of place: its materials, its layered histories, and its human rhythms.

This approach is equally at home in the conservation of a 14th-century Benedictine monastery and the design of contemporary residential complexes, because the discipline is always the same — attentive observation, honest materials, and forms that belong.

I
Anticipate

Reading trends in contemporary architecture to inform each project's future relevance.

II
Research

Deep material and contextual analysis before a single line is drawn.

III
Collaborate

Walking every phase of the design process alongside the client, as true partners.

IV
Realise

Delivering work that respects environment, current norms, and energy efficiency.
